A Journey of Love

Our Story

From a chance meeting to a lifetime promise — this is how our love began.

2019
🌸

First Meeting

Fate brought two souls together at the University of Colombo during the Faculty of Science orientation. A simple smile across the lecture hall changed everything forever.

2020
☕

First Date

A rainy evening in Kandy, a warm cup of tea, and hours of laughter. They both knew this was the beginning of something truly beautiful and rare.

2022
🌊

Adventures Together

From the misty mountains of Ella to the golden shores of Mirissa, they explored the beauty of Sri Lanka hand in hand, building memories for a lifetime.

2024
💍

The Proposal

Under the stars at Galle Fort, with the ocean breeze and the scent of jasmine in the air, Anuradha asked the question that would begin their forever. She said yes.

2026
🌺

The Wedding Day

Now, surrounded by the love of family and friends, Anuradha and Sehani begin their forever on the 14th of September 2026. And you are part of this beautiful chapter.
